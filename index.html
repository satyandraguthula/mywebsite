Research

Network data is abundant but labels are scarce, and most ML-for-networking models are trained for a single task on a single dataset. My work asks whether we can pretrain once on large volumes of unlabeled traffic and reuse the result across many tasks and networks. Current threads:

  • Foundation models for packet traces. netFound learns hierarchical representations of packets, bursts and flows from 1 TB of raw traces.
  • Bursty, intermittent time series. NetBurst models traffic as a sequence of events, which suits the sparse, heavy-tailed signals that general-purpose forecasters handle poorly.
  • Evaluating what these models learn. Probing pretrained network foundation models without relying on downstream tasks.
